Reading the board

Every row is scored by one function of the snapshot's own figures. Ranks are a total order: no two rows share an implied value. The watchlist is the floor, the early rows are the pre-token protocols below it, and the live-token protocols are set aside because their market cap is a fact rather than a model.

  1. Sort by implied value to see the strongest launch case first.
  2. Filter to one category or chain to compare like with like.
  3. Open a row for its multipliers and its six-step farm plan.
  4. Read the implied value as an estimate, never as a price.
