Reading the board

Every row is one function of the snapshot's own figures. Ranks are a total order: no two rows share an implied value. The watchlist is the floor, the early rows are the pre-token protocols below it, and the live-token protocols are set aside because their market cap is a fact rather than a model.

  1. Search by name or slug, or filter to one category.
  2. Sort by TVL, 7-day change or implied value.
  3. Open a row's model toggle to see the four multipliers behind it.
  4. Select a row for its full breakdown and its numbered farm plan.
